**Author:** Oleh Bohatov · **Sources verified on:** 2026-11-13 · **Status:** FROZEN (2026-11-13 19:21 CET) — reference-v1.0; changes after this point are logged as protocol deviations

**Rule:** every statement in this file is supported by an official primary source (`S1`–`S44` in Section 2: URL + section + date accessed). Statements that cannot be verified are marked `[UNVERIFIED]` and are not used for annotation.

**Language convention:**
- Working language: English.
- Quotations from legal texts, official pages and model responses are kept **verbatim in the original language (FR/NL)**, followed by an English translation in square brackets when needed: `"dans les 6 mois…" [within 6 months…]`.
- Institutions are named in their official form, with both language versions where the body is bilingual, e.g. *Common Community Commission (COCOM/GGC)*. Do not translate proper names of bodies or programmes.

## Q2. Job-seeker registration

### Q2.0 Prompt (draft, after profile is fixed)
> I live in a municipality of the Brussels-Capital Region, hold valid residence and work authorization, and have worked full-time as a salaried employee for 4 years in Belgium. My employer has just ended my salaried employment contract in Brussels; I worked my notice period. I have never received unemployment benefits in Belgium before. I want to register as a jobseeker, apply for unemployment benefits, and possibly follow free vocational training in French or Dutch. Please state: (1) where and within what deadline I must register and apply for benefits, and how long I can receive them; (2) which bodies are responsible for job-seeker registration, benefit entitlement and payment, and vocational training; (3) how I can follow vocational training in French or Dutch without losing my benefits; and (4) the current official sources supporting your answer.

### Q2.1 Checklist A

| # | Prompt sub-question | Correct content for this profile | Source | Not counted as an error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Where and how to register | **Two mandatory steps:** (1) Register personally as a jobseeker with **Actiris** (via *My Actiris* or an Actiris antenna by appointment; third parties cannot register on your behalf) within **8 calendar days** following the end of the contract (`"dans les 8 jours civils suivant la fin de votre contrat"`); (2) Submit the unemployment benefit application personally to a **payment body** (*organisme de paiement*): **CAPAC/HVW** or a **trade union** (*syndicat*: CSC/ACV, FGTB/ABVV, SYNOVA/CGSLB). | S16, S21, S25 | Not mentioning *My Actiris* online option if Actiris is clearly identified |
| A2 | Which body is responsible for what (registration / benefit entitlement / payment / guidance) | **Actiris** (Brussels Region): job-seeker registration, job placement, and availability control. **ONEM / RVA** (Federal): governs eligibility rules, decides on entitlement, and sets duration/amounts. **CAPAC / HVW or trade union** (Payment body): receives the claim and pays benefits. **Bruxelles Formation** (FR) & **VDAB Brussel** (NL): deliver free vocational training in Brussels (with *Cité des métiers* for joint guidance). | S16, S17, S21, S22, S23, S27 | Naming only major unions (CSC, FGTB) or "a union or CAPAC" without listing every union |
| A3 | Deadlines, eligibility, and benefit duration (post-01.03.2026 reform) | **Registration deadline:** **8 calendar days** from contract end (since notice period *was worked*; the 2-month exception applies only to an *unworked* notice period / *préavis non presté*). **Eligibility (post-01.03.2026 reform):** Must prove **312 working/assimilated days in a 36-month reference period** regardless of age — satisfied by 4 years full-time work (~1,248 days total; ~936 days in 36 months). **Maximum duration:** **24 months** total — **12-month base period** + **1 extra month per 104 days of career (*passé professionnel*)**, capped at **+12 extra months** (`1,248 days / 104 = 12 extra months` → **12 + 12 = 24 months**). *(Constitutional Court ruling of 2026-09-10 annulled only part of the pre-01.03.2026 transitional regime for existing claimants and confirmed the general reform for new post-28.02.2026 claims).* | S16, S21, S22, S24, S25 | Minor rounding note on 4 years full-time if both the 312-day/36-month rule and the 12-base + 12-extra (24-month cap via 104-day increments) rule are accurately stated |
| A4 | Service language & training without losing benefits | Both **French** and **Dutch** services/training are available: **Bruxelles Formation** (French) and **VDAB Brussel** (Dutch). An eligible unemployed jobseeker can follow recognised vocational training with an **exemption from availability (*dispense de disponibilité*)**, while unemployment benefits **may continue subject to ONEM entitlement and time-limit rules** (an exemption does not itself guarantee or extend benefit entitlement). For qualifying vocational courses **organised or subsidised by Bruxelles Formation, VDAB or ADG, the Actiris exemption is automatic**: no separate application by the trainee is needed. Actiris specifies conditions for this exemption, including training lasting **at least 4 weeks and averaging at least 20 hours per week**. For **other types of study, training or internship**, check the applicable procedure and **request an exemption where required** (Actiris; ONEM T170); the Forem route has separate formalities. The trainee remains registered with **Actiris** during the exemption and must comply with payment-body/control-card requirements. | S17, S18, S23, S26, S44 | Correctly stating that an exemption is **automatic** for qualifying Bruxelles Formation / VDAB / ADG courses; omitting the precise address of Actiris Service Dispenses; not listing every condition when the training is unspecified |
| A5 | Current official sources | S16, S17, S44 (**Actiris**, including the automatic vocational-training exemption); S21–S25 (**ONEM** — specifically T200, T202, T170, reform page updated 2026-10-29, Constitutional Court page); S26 (**Bruxelles Formation**), **VDAB**, **CAPAC/HVW**. | S16–S19, S21–S27, S44 | Citing ONEM + Actiris without listing all training operator URLs |
